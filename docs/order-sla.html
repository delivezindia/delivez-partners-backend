Order SLA — what happens when an order doesn’t go to plan.

Every order has a time limit at each stage. When an order is cancelled or a delivery fails, the system records why (a reason code) and when (the stage). From those two it decides whose fault it was, what the customer gets back, what Delivez pays the shop, and what happens to the goods. Nothing is decided by hand except the reason ops pick.

Each stage has a clock

Breaches are checked every 5 seconds. Alerts fire once per order.

The three principles

  • The customer pays only for their own choices after the shop started work.
  • The shop is never out of pocket for cooked food it couldn’t sell, unless it caused the cancel.
  • Goods that can go back on a shelf always go back.

Who can cancel

  • Customer: in the app, only before the shop accepts (free).
  • Shop: rejects before accepting, with a reason.
  • Ops: any time before pickup, must pick a reason code.
  • After pickup: no cancel — it’s “Delivery failed” with a reason.

Records kept

  • Reason code, fault and stage on every ended order.
  • Store compensation and customer unpaid amounts.
  • Shop-fault ends count on the store record; customer-fault failures after work started are recorded against the customer.

5 · Who sees what

On each screen

Customer app

  • The reason in plain words, in their language (21 reasons × 8 languages).
  • “Sorry — it wasn’t your fault” when it wasn’t.
  • Money line: refund amount and 5–10 working days, delivery fee kept, not charged, or unpaid amount.
  • Link to the refund & cancellation policy.

Partners app (shop)

  • “Why this order ended” card with the reason.
  • “Delivez will pay you ₹… for the food you made.”
  • “This counts on your store record” for shop-fault ends.
  • Goods coming back: check in as back on shelf / damaged.
  • Alerts: cancelled (with compensation), running late, delivery failed.

Admin console (ops)

  • Cancel = pick a reason; each option shows its outcome before you confirm.
  • Delivery failed = pick a reason.
  • Order panel: fault, refunded, store compensation, unpaid.
  • Alerts: stuck in kitchen, no rider, rider waiting at the door.

6 · Open points for approval

Decisions needed

Not built yet (needs a decision or another module)

  • Paying the shop: compensation is recorded per order; actual payout comes with the payouts module.
  • Refunds: recorded as “refund initiated”; real money moves when Razorpay is connected (L5).
  • Online payment: app orders are paid by UPI, card or net banking. Until Razorpay is connected the payment is simulated in dev/test and app ordering is switched off in production.
  • Customer push alerts for cancellations come with L3; today the order screen shows it.

Refund policy now matches this SLA

  • The customer policy page (/refund-policy) was rewritten from these rules: free cancel only before the shop accepts, no cancellation fees, no waiting charges, no wallet credits, no self-pickup.
  • Commitments to confirm before launch: 24 h to report a problem with food or medicines, 48 h support reply, and the support email (placeholder: support@delivez.com).
