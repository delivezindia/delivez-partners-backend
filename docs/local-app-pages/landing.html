Why Delivez

Made for how your neighbourhood shops

Shops you already know

Your neighbourhood restaurants, kiranas and chemists — not far-away warehouses.

Pay the way you like

Cash or UPI to the rider, or pay at the shop when you pick up.

Check at your door

Grocery and shop orders: the rider waits 5 minutes while you check. Hand back anything damaged or wrong and it’s refunded.

A code for every order

Your order is handed over only when you share its 4-digit code — at the door or the counter.

Cancel for free

Changed your mind? Cancel at no charge until the shop accepts your order.

In your language

Use Delivez in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali.

Always in the loop

Know where your order is

Every order follows the same five steps, and the order screen updates as the shop and rider move.

  1. Placed

    Your order reaches the shop instantly.

  2. Confirmed by the shop

    The shop confirms and says when it will be ready.

  3. Preparing

    Your food is cooked, or your items packed.

  4. On the way

    A Delivez rider picks it up and heads to you.

  5. Delivered

    Share your code at the door. Groceries: check your items before the rider leaves.

Health, handled with care

Medicines and lab tests too

From the pharmacies and labs you trust, with the checks the law asks for.

💊

Medicines

  • Licensed pharmacies only — documents are checked before a pharmacy goes live.
  • Prescription medicines are checked by a registered pharmacist on duty before the order is accepted.
  • Upload your prescription in the app — coming soon.
🧪

Lab tests

  • Labs are verified, including their accreditation, before they take bookings.
  • See the sample type, fasting and report time before you book.
  • Home sample collection where the lab offers it. Slot booking in the app — coming soon.

Your prescriptions are seen only by the pharmacy handling your order.
