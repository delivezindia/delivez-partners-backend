  • Shops, items, prices and the order are sample data from our test setup (Demo Grocery, Meghana Foods). The order code and times change on every order.
  • The map on "Add address" is a picture of the live Google map.
  • Payment today is cash / UPI on delivery or at the shop; online payment, prescription upload and lab slot booking are planned next.
